Online store: context for the support agent

1. Business process and organization
The Refunds desk is part of Customer Care. Dana Ortiz heads Customer Care.
Refunds over $500 must be approved by the Finance controller, unless the customer is on the VIP list.
Refund review:
1. Check the order status.
2. If the item was damaged, issue the refund.
3. Otherwise, ask the customer to return the item.

2. Domain meaning
In Marketing, a customer is anyone with an account. In Finance, a customer is anyone who has paid at least one invoice.
Shoppers is another word for customers.
Each customer can place many orders.

3. Data products
The Customer 360 data product combines the customers and orders tables. The Data team owns Customer 360.
Customer 360 is approved for marketing segmentation.
Repeat purchase rate is the number of customers with two or more orders divided by the number of customers.

4. Physical systems
The customers table lives in Snowflake as SHOP.CORE.CUSTOMERS, one row per customer.
The F_NAME column holds the customer's first name.
Orders join to customers on orders.customer_id = customers.id.

5. Tools and integration
The issue_refund tool issues a refund through the payments system.
Only the Refunds desk may use issue_refund.
Never use issue_refund for orders older than 90 days.
Sample call: issue_refund(order_id, amount, reason).
