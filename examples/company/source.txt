Data
The Data team owns the Customer 360 data product, which combines the customers and orders tables.
The customers table has the columns customer_id, email and signup_date.
Priya Nair advises on Customer 360.
The orders table has the columns order_id, customer_id, order_date, amount and status. Each order is identified by order_id.
Orders join to customers on orders.customer_id = customers.customer_id.
The refunds table has the columns refund_id, order_id and refund_amount. Refunds join to orders on refunds.order_id = orders.order_id.
The orders table is built from the raw_orders table, which is loaded from the shop database.

Metrics
Revenue is the sum of amount in the orders table, excluding cancelled orders.
Order count is the number of distinct order_id values in the orders table.
Average order value is revenue divided by order count.
Refund amount is the sum of refund_amount in the refunds table.
Net revenue is revenue minus refund amount.
More website traffic increases order count.

Escalation
The Returns desk escalates to the Fulfillment team head, who escalates to the Operations head.

Rules
Operations must log every refund in the refund register.
Standard shipping rules: the Fulfillment team must ship orders within two days, unless the item is on backorder.
Peak-season rules: the Fulfillment team must ship orders within four days.
Peak-season rules override the standard shipping rules.
The Returns desk may approve refunds up to $200.
Only the Returns desk may use the refund tool.
