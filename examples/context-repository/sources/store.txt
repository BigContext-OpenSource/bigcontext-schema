Acme is an online store. The Operations team and the Support team are part of Acme.
Dana leads the Operations team.

The orders table has one row per order. Its columns are ORDER_ID, ORDER_DATE and STATUS.
The shipments table has one row per shipment. Its columns are SHIPMENT_ID and ORDER_ID.
The shipments table joins to the orders table on ORDER_ID.

Orders is the number of orders placed in a period, counted by ORDER_DATE.
Customers must receive a text message when their order goes out for delivery.

When an order is late:
1. Check the carrier status.
2. Tell the customer what happened.

For Support, Orders is the number of orders placed in a period whose STATUS is Shipped.
