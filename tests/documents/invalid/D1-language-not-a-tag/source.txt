Sales data notes
Each order joins the customer_history row with its customer_id whose valid_from and valid_to include the order date.
Each order joins the latest fx_rates row on or before its order date, for its currency_code.
orders.amount is in the currency given by orders.currency_code.
Umsatz is the German word for revenue.
employees.manager_id points to the employee's manager, to any depth.
events is partitioned by event_date.
Active customers are customers whose status is active.
A price applies to an order placed on or after its valid_from and before its valid_until.
Duplicate customers are found by joining customers to itself on the same email and a different customer_id.
The last 3 days of sales may still change, because orders can arrive late.
The refund data for the previous fiscal quarter is incomplete.
Our fiscal year starts on the Sunday nearest 1 February and follows a 4-4-5 pattern.
Each fiscal week begins on a Monday, and a sales day begins at 04:00.
The Order Completed event is recorded when a payment is captured.
The churn model is retrained every month.
Returns have a minor effect on the forecast.
