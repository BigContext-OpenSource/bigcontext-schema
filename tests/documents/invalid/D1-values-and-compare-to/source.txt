Order tracking policy (online store)

Customers must receive a text message when their order goes out for delivery.
Standard orders usually ship within two business days, unless the item is on backorder.
Express and Standard together make up the shipping options.
The Fulfillment team is part of Operations and is responsible for shipping.
The Support team answers delivery questions.

When an order is late:
1. Check the carrier status.
2. If the parcel is lost, reship the order.
3. Tell the customer what happened.
