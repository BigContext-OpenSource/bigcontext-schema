The orders table has one row per order.
ORDER_ID identifies a row of the orders table.
CUSTOMER_ID identifies a row of the customers table.
An order is one purchase, identified by ORDER_ID of the orders table.
A customer is someone who has bought from us, identified by CUSTOMER_ID of the customers table.
The orders table joins to the customers table on CUSTOMER_ID of the orders table.
Orders is the number of rows of the orders table.
City is the CITY column of the customers table.
