Shop sales notes

Sales adds up AMOUNT in the orders table, by ORDER_DATE.
The order count counts each ORDER_ID in the orders table once.
A test order is one whose IS_TEST is 1.
Test orders are never included in sales.
A large order is one whose AMOUNT is over 500.
Promotion orders carry a PROMOTION_ID.
An order is placed during its promotion when its ORDER_DATE falls from the promotion's STARTS_ON to its ENDS_ON.
Promotion sales adds up AMOUNT over promotion orders.
A promotion order counts in promotion sales only if it was placed during its promotion.
Do not use DISCOUNT_AMOUNT in sales.
Average basket is sales over the order count, for the orders counted in sales.
The regions are North and South, and REGION in the orders table holds an order's region.
Sales the same week last year is sales one year back, by week.
The weekly change is sales against the same week last year, in percent.
Tell Merchandising when a region's weekly change is over 10%.
Our fiscal year starts on 1 February.
Sales for fiscal Q1 2026 were signed off.
Nobody edits signed-off sales without the controller's approval.
Compare both years on the current definition of sales.
Each row of the returns table belongs to one order, by its ORDER_ID.
A damaged return is one whose REASON is damaged.
Sales does not take off returns.
Kept sales adds up AMOUNT in the orders table, taking 0 for an order whose STATUS is sent back.
Each order belongs to one row of the customers table, by its CUSTOMER_ID.
Member sales adds up AMOUNT over the orders of customers whose TIER is member.
A promotion order belongs to one row of the promotions table, by its PROMOTION_ID.
Signed-off sales carry the mark final in every report.
Sales are written in whole dollars with thousands separators.
